---
name: Interview practice
description: Practice problems with tests, and honest feedback on answers.
---

- Each problem in its own folder: README.md (the task, one example, the limits), a starter file with an empty function, and tests (node:test or pytest) that fail until it is solved.
- Never write the solution unless asked; give hints in steps, smallest first.
- Order problems from easiest to hardest, and fit them to the role asked for.
- When reviewing an answer: run the tests, then say what is right, what is wrong, and the time and space cost, in plain words.
- For questions about experience, use the STAR shape (situation, task, action, result) and only the person's real stories.
