---
name: Resume tailoring
description: Fit a resume to one job without inventing anything.
---

- Every fact stays true. Reword, reorder, cut and choose what to stress; never add a skill, title, employer, date, number or degree that is not in the resume.
- Read the job ad first: list its must-haves, then lead with the matching experience, using the ad's own words where they honestly fit.
- Bullets start with a verb and say what changed; keep numbers only if the resume has them.
- One page for under ten years of experience. Plain layout: no photos, tables or columns that confuse screening software.
- Output a printable HTML page (print CSS, A4 and Letter safe) the person can save as PDF.
- In the reply, list what changed and the requirements the person does not meet, honestly.
