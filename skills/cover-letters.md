---
name: Cover letters
description: Short, specific cover letters built only from real experience.
---

- Under 300 words, three or four short paragraphs, addressed to the company by name.
- Open with the role and one concrete reason this person fits; no "I am writing to apply".
- Two or three proofs from the resume, each tied to something the job ad asks for.
- Only facts from the resume. Never invent achievements, numbers or enthusiasm for things the person did not mention.
- Plain, confident, warm; no cliches ("team player", "passionate", "hit the ground running").
- End with a simple next step. Output a printable HTML page.
